How it works运作逻辑

Connect investment with real products.连接资本参与与真实产品。

Investment-led procurement and distributor purchases support demand. The company remains responsible for supplying the products.投资带动的采购与经销商采购共同支持产品需求，上市公司按合同承担供货与履约责任。

  1. 01 / PARTICIPATE参与

    Eligible investors合资格投资者

    Subscribe for PR+ to hold product rights with contractually agreed upside.认购 PR+，按条款持有产品权益及约定上行收益。

  2. 02 / PROCURE采购

    Issuing vehicle (SPV)发行载体（SPV）

    Purchases and locks FPR, and holds a separate upside contract.购买并锁定 FPR，同时持有独立的收益合约。

  3. 03 / FULFIL履约

    Listed company上市公司

    Supplies products as agreed. FPR converts to PR as obligations are fulfilled.按约定提供产品，FPR 随履约转为 PR。

  4. 04 / PURCHASE购买

    Distributors & buyers经销商与终端客户

    Purchase available product rights and take delivery, supporting sales and repeat demand.采购可流通的产品权益并提货，推动销售与持续复购。

FPR · Forward Product Rights预售权益Rights to future products under agreed delivery terms.按约定交付条件取得未来商品的权益。

PR · Product Rights商品权益Rights to take delivery of available goods.按约定提取已生产商品的权益。

PR+ · Enhanced securities product增强型证券产品FPR combined with contractually agreed upside.FPR 与约定上行收益相结合的证券产品。

Supporting roles: qualified partners provide distribution, custody, trading and professional advice; contract counterparties provide the agreed upside. Rights locked within PR+ cannot circulate separately until released under the agreed terms.专业分工：由合资格伙伴提供分销、托管、交易及专业意见，收益合约提供方承担约定的收益结算义务。已锁入 PR+ 的权益须按约定解除锁定后，方可独立流通。
